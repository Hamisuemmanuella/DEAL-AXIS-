How it works

Three steps. One close.

Our process is simple by design. The complexity is on our side — not yours.

01

Submit or register

Sellers submit deal details confidentially. Buyers register their acquisition criteria. Everything stays private until we confirm a match exists.

02

We verify and match

We verify the deal, check documentation, qualify both parties, and match from our buyer network. NDA signed before any introduction is made.

03

Deal closes, fee paid

We facilitate the introduction and support negotiation. Our fee is paid only upon successful closing — our incentives are perfectly aligned with yours.

Common Questions
